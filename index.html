Prepare 4 3W

Version 1.2.1

A small toolbox that helps you prepare assets before publishing a website. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is ever uploaded.

Currently included: a favicon generator and Web Card House, a header/main/footer builder (beta).

Photo picker — fast, but SVGs and other non-photo files may not show up here.

or drag and drop it here
or paste it from your clipboard (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
